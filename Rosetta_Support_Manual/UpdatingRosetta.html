Signal Tower Relays


Signal Tower Relay Connections

Key Notes

The connections to the Mesa 7i92 is based on the Mesa 7i92TH Pin Mapping.

This is based on a relay operating at +5 VDC.

There are other relays which operate at +3 to 3.3 VDC, or other voltages. The red VCC line will need to be adjusted to match the relay’s voltage requirements.
